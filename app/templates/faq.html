General & Search Architecture

How does GrantFinder AI discover opportunities without returning expired listings?

GrantFinder AI deploys a hybrid retrieval architecture. A core relational database caches verified institutional programs (such as HEC, Ignite, USEFP, and DAAD) that are reviewed periodically. Whenever you trigger a search, the system concurrently queries live Serper.dev web indexes to extract recently announced cohorts and active deadlines. This guarantees you never rely on stale forum threads or dead links.

How is the 0-100% Match Score calculated?

The platform evaluates your profile across multiple orthogonal factors: field of study or venture industry, target degree stage or venture maturity, geographic eligibility, GPA cutoff alignment, and financial need. Google Gemini analyzes these parameters against the opportunity criteria and returns a deterministic percentage score alongside a written rationale explaining why you qualify.

Student Scholarships

What degree levels and programs are supported?

The platform supports Undergraduate (Bachelor's), Postgraduate (Master's), Doctoral (PhD), and Post-Doctoral research fellowships. Fields span Computer Science, Engineering, Medicine, Business, Humanities, Arts, and Social Sciences.

How does the AI Statement of Purpose (SOP) Drafter work?

When you select a scholarship opportunity, our AI drafting engine ingests your academic milestones, target program, research aspirations, and background. It structures a tailored 4-paragraph academic statement formatted according to international university admission standards.

Category 03 •

Startup & Founder Grants

What does non-dilutive equity-free grant mean?

Non-dilutive capital refers to financial awards and prototype development grants that do not require you to surrender equity, board seats, or future ownership in your company. Programs from bodies like Ignite, NIC, and Karandaaz award these grants to foster technological innovation without taking founder equity.

How does the AI Pitch Proposal Drafter assist early-stage founders?

The Pitch Drafter formats your raw venture concept into a 5-section executive proposal meeting committee grant standards: Executive Summary, Problem Validation, Solution & Technology, Traction & Milestones, and Fund Utilization & Budget Breakdown.

Category 04 •

Account Security & Verification

Why is 6-digit email OTP verification mandatory upon registration?

To prevent malicious account creation and protect your saved opportunities and application drafts, all registrations require email verification. A secure 6-digit code is dispatched via Gmail SMTP. Unverified users cannot bypass the verification screen or access portal features until their email ownership is confirmed.

What happens if my OTP code expires or is incorrect?

Each OTP code remains valid for 10 minutes. If entered incorrectly 5 times, it is invalidated to prevent brute force attempts. You can click "Resend Code" after a 60-second cooldown timer to receive a fresh verification code.

How can I reset my password if I forget it?

Navigate to the Login page and click "Forgot Password?". Enter your registered email to receive a secure, time-limited reset link. The reset token expires in 15 minutes and automatically invalidates your prior credentials upon completion.
