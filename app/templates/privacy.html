Institutional Data Protection

Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: October 8, 2026. GrantFinder AI is committed to safeguarding the personal, academic, and venture information entrusted to us by applicants, researchers, and student founders.

1. Information We Collect

To provide targeted opportunity matching and AI-powered drafting assistance, we collect information you explicitly provide:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified institutional or personal email address, and cryptographically hashed passwords (using PBKDF2 with SHA-256).
  • Academic & Venture Profiles: Field of study, major domain, degree level, GPA or academic standing, startup industry sector, venture stage, and target destination preferences.
  • Application Artifacts: Draft Statements of Purpose, research summaries, and grant pitch narratives stored securely for your personal reference.
  • Technical Telemetry: Log timestamps, HTTP security audit headers, and session identifiers necessary for platform security and rate limiting.

2. How We Utilize Your Data

Your data is processed strictly for the following platform capabilities:

  • Computing semantic compatibility scores between your profile and live scholarship or grant opportunities.
  • Delivering mandatory account verification codes (OTP) and critical system notifications via secure SMTP pipelines.
  • Synthesizing personalized application essays and grant proposals via isolated Google Gemini LLM API calls.
  • Preventing malicious bot activity, unauthorized access attempts, and brute-force authentication attacks.

3. Zero Sale and Data Minimization Commitment

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or academic information to third-party advertisers or commercial brokers.

Data shared with underlying infrastructure providers (such as Supabase for persistent relational storage and Serper for web searches) is scoped strictly to the minimal parameters required to execute requested actions.

4. Cryptographic Security & OTP Protection

All account verification one-time passwords (OTPs) are stored exclusively as SHA-256 cryptographic hashes and verified using constant-time string comparisons. Plaintext OTP tokens are immediately discarded from server memory and are never exposed in API payloads or browser logs.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You retain complete control over your profile data. At any time, you may update your profile preferences, clear your saved bookmarks, or request complete account deletion by contacting our governance team at privacy@grantfinder.ai.
