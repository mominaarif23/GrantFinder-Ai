How does the Hybrid Data Model prevent outdated results?

Major, stable government sources (HEC, Ignite, Karandaaz, PSEB) are curated into our database. Whenever you execute a search, the system concurrently queries live Serper.dev web indexes to extract newly announced cohorts, ensuring you never encounter expired deadlines.

What is included in the $9 one-time Premium demonstration tier?

Premium removes the 3-result cutoff on all searches, unlocks unlimited opportunity matches, enables the Gemini-powered AI Statement of Purpose and Pitch Proposal drafters, and activates real-time WhatsApp instant alerts.

Can I use GrantFinder AI for international study and global accelerator grants?

Yes. The platform indexes opportunities across Pakistan, the United Kingdom, the United States, Germany, Australia, and worldwide programs including Fulbright, Chevening, DAAD, Erasmus Mundus, and Y Combinator.
