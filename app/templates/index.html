How do you make sure the scholarships are still open and accepting applications?

We keep a verified list of major government scholarship programs updated regularly. When you run a search, we also check live listings so you see the most recent announcements — including deadlines for the current semester.

What do I get with the $9 upgrade?

The $9 one-time upgrade removes the 3-result limit on every search, so you see all matching opportunities. It also unlocks the essay writing helper for students and the grant proposal helper for founders, plus WhatsApp alerts when deadlines are approaching.

Can I find scholarships for studying abroad, or grants for my startup?

Yes. We cover programs in Pakistan (HEC, Ignite, PSEB), the UK (Chevening, Rhodes), the US (Fulbright), Germany (DAAD), Australia (Australia Awards), and global programs like Erasmus Mundus and Gates Cambridge — as well as startup competitions and accelerators.
